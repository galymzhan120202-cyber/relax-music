# Privacy Policy — CrimsonRoom Auto-Pipeline

Last updated: 2026-09-05

CrimsonRoom is a personal, non-commercial automation tool that generates
and uploads music videos to a single YouTube channel owned by the
developer. It is not a public-facing product and does not have
third-party end users.

## What data this app accesses

This app uses the YouTube Data API v3 (`youtube.upload` scope) solely to
upload video files to the developer's own YouTube channel on a
scheduled basis. It does not read, collect, share, or sell any personal
data belonging to viewers, channel subscribers, or any third party.

## What data this app does NOT do

- It does not access any Google account other than the one explicitly
  authorized by the developer.
- It does not read emails, contacts, files, or any other Google
  account data.
- It does not share any data with third parties.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at the email
address listed on the associated Google Cloud project's OAuth consent
screen.
